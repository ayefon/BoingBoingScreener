Support

Getting started, common questions, and how to get in touch.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad running iOS 26 or later, or a Mac running macOS 26 or later
  • A Tradier API token for live market data (a free sandbox token gives delayed data, which is fine for this daily screen; a production token comes with a Tradier brokerage account)

Getting Started

1. Add your Tradier token

Open Settings → Tradier, choose the Account type that matches your token (Sandbox or Production), and paste the token. It’s stored only in your device’s Keychain.

2. Choose what to scan

In Settings → Screen, choose bullish, bearish or both, and each side’s price band ($20 to $500 to start). Settings → Universes picks the lists to scan; the S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 are on to start.

3. Run a scan

Tap Scan. Candidates appear as they’re found: bullish ones with long calls, bearish ones with long puts. Tap one for its chart, 2-day RSI, IV gauge and example options. Stocks that fit the chart rules but not the option checks are listed below the candidates, with the reason.

4. Not ready for a token yet?

Tap Try with Sample Data on the start screen (or in Settings → Tradier) to explore the whole app with made-up prices.

FAQ

A scan says “Add your Tradier API token”, or Tradier rejected the token

Check that the Account setting matches the token: sandbox tokens only work with Sandbox, and production tokens only with Production. Use the eye button in Settings to check the saved token for typos.

Almost everything fails the option checks

Outside regular market hours, option bid/ask spreads are often much wider than during the session, so more stocks fail the liquidity test, and the list says so. Scan during market hours for the truest quotes.

No candidates at all

Some days few stocks fit: the rules need a sharp 2-day move against a trend. Try more universes, or check the price bands in Settings → Screen.

A symbol never appears

Share-class tickers use a slash for Tradier (for example BRK/B for BRK.B). Check the symbol’s Tradier form in Settings → Universes → Manage Universes. Very new or thinly traded stocks may have no options 28 or more days out. To check any symbol against the rules, type it in the Look up symbols field at the top of the list.

The RSI doesn’t match my charting app

The app uses TradingView’s 2-day RSI (Wilder’s smoothing) on Tradier’s daily closes. Charts that adjust past prices for dividends can show slightly different values.

What is the IV gauge?

It shows where the option market’s 30-day implied volatility sits compared with how much the stock has actually moved over the past year, as a section from 1 to 5 and a rank. A scan’s candidates all read below the top section. The gauge needs options around 30 days out and about 14 months of prices, so recently listed stocks have none yet.

A zone I expected isn’t on the chart

The chart shows only zones price hasn’t closed through, found back as far as Settings → Charts → Zone Search reaches (a year unless you change it, up to two). Drag the strip under the chart to scroll back, drag the prices at the left to look above or below, or check the Nearest supply and Nearest demand lines under the chart.

Can I send the candidates to another app?

Yes. The Export menu in the list’s toolbar has Copy Tickers (one comma-separated line), Share Symbols (a CSV file per side, one symbol per line, for apps that import watchlists) and Share Results (a spreadsheet per side with the scan’s figures).

How do I switch between different screen settings?

Save them as a settings set. At the top of Settings, tap Save Current Settings As… to save the Screen settings and the selected universes under a name. Load a saved set from Saved Sets; each set’s ⋯ button renames, updates, or deletes it. Sets are saved on your device only.

How do I get the default settings back?

Use Restore Default Screen in Settings → Screen. There’s no need to delete the app. A saved settings set keeps the values it was saved with until you update it.

What’s kept if I delete the app?

On an iPhone or iPad, deleting the app erases everything it saved on that device: your universes and your edits to them, settings sets, bookmarks, and every setting. A reinstall starts from the defaults with the preloaded universes. Your Tradier token is kept in the Keychain and usually comes back after reinstalling; tap Remove in Settings → Tradier first if you don’t want that. On the Mac, deleting the app keeps everything, settings included.

Does the app place trades?

No. Boing Boing Screener only reads market data. It never places orders, reads account balances or positions, or moves money.
